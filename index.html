The Beginning  ·  Origin
Where It All Started

N/S Face was born from a simple but powerful idea — that Ghana deserves a streetwear brand built from the inside out. Founded by Abraham Atiah and Ayman Gundona, two creatives driven btothers a shared vision, uzi mvmt5 is not just clothing. It is a declaration that the streets of Ghana have a voice, a style, and a story worth wearing.

The Mission  ·  Why We Exist
Rule the Streets with Fashion and Love

Our motto is simple: Rule the Streets with Fashion and Love. Every piece we design carries that dual energy — the sharpness of street culture and the warmth of genuine human connection. Fashion is not just about looking good. It is about feeling seen, feeling proud, and moving through the world with intention.

The Aesthetic  ·  Our Language
Ghanaian Roots. Global Ambition.

N/S Face blends the raw energy of street culture with the rich identity of Ghana. Oversized silhouettes, heavyweight fabrics, bold graphic language — every design decision is intentional. We are not copying trends from elsewhere. We are creating the trend, right here, rooted in who we are and where we come from.

Right Now  ·  Brand New
Just Getting Started

uzi mvmt is a brand-new movement. We are in our earliest chapter — building, creating, and laying the foundation for something that will last. We are not yet shipping orders anywhere, but the world is about to meet us. Watch this space. The best is coming, and it is coming from Ghana.
